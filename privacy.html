Privacy Policy

Effective July 16, 2026

Row Echelon is a puzzle game with optional online player profiles and a daily leaderboard. This policy explains the limited information used to provide those features.

Information we use

  • The username you choose, which is visible on the leaderboard.
  • Gameplay results such as points, levels solved, steps, completion time, and saved puzzle progress.
  • A random session token stored on your device to keep the current profile active.

Why we use it

This information is used only to operate player sessions, restore a username's progress, calculate rankings, display the leaderboard, and prevent abusive usernames. Row Echelon does not use advertising, sell personal information, or track activity across other apps or websites.

Retention and visibility

Daily points reset each day. Lifetime solved totals and the profile name remain so returning players can continue with the same profile. Profiles with zero current-day points are not displayed on the daily leaderboard. A player session times out after 30 minutes without activity.

Your choices

Do not use personal information in a public username. You can end the active session from Settings. For help with profiles, saved progress, or leaderboard behavior, visit the Row Echelon support page.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
